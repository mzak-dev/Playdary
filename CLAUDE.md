# Playdary

- Commits: Angular-style conventional commits, subject only, no body, no AI attribution in commits or PRs.
- Code: self-explaining names, at most one line of comments; longer notes go to `docs/`.
- Layout: `apps/web` (Vite + React + TanStack Router), `apps/backend` (Supabase), `packages/design-system` (tokens + components).
